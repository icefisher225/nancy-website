Stage 8: O Porrino to Arcade

Published Monday, September 21, 2026

Data: 15.75 miles, 2,103 feet of elevation, 5:51 moving time, 7:41 actual time on trail.
Hotel: Hotel Arcade

A 6 AM start time today with the stars twinkling in the night sky. The roosters, dogs, and sunrise took turns waking up the village. Bread deliveries were underway in the village and we made way for their speedy uturns. Our early morning jaunt also included walking be a recently “painted” door in red. The assumption is that some employee or political group is unhappy with the company CEO. It would be interesting to know the story…

It was a grueling day with steep ascents and descents, but always beautiful. The uphill climbs were long and seemingly endless. A special stamp on the trail was a nice reward for reaching the summit by noon.

Prior to starting the climb we rounded a corner to see pilgrims lined up for the only Albergue in the area. The next one was 8 miles away—a reminder to those who stay in alburgues—never a guarantee for a bed. (We are in hotels.) reservations are key.

Claire and I had a delicious dinner of cod, scallops, and vino blanc with pistachio cake for dessert. My first dessert since the start of our adventure! So good!

My feet are cooked at the end of this long day. But my spirit is soaring! Great day on the trail. This is an experience like no other…
